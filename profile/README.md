## 👋 Who we are

**Fincept Corporation** builds open-source financial intelligence tools for investors, analysts, and portfolio managers — the kind of institutional-grade tooling that has historically sat behind $20,000+/year terminals.

We believe real-time market data, deep research, and AI-driven analysis should be accessible to **every curious mind** — not just the desks that can afford Bloomberg. Everything we ship is open, cross-platform, and built for professionals and self-directed investors alike.
